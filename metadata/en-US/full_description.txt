BadNotes is a notebook for writing by hand on your phone or tablet.

• Pens that feel right: fountain, ballpoint, brush, calligraphy, pencil and highlighter, with pressure on pens that report it.
• Paper your way: plain, lined, squared, dotted and more, in several colours.
• Write on PDFs and photos, add sticky notes, text boxes and shapes.
• Record audio while you write and play it back with the ink.
• Find things in your handwriting.
• Keep your notes on your device, or sync them with your own Google Drive or OneDrive.

No account with us, no ads, no tracking.
