# BadNotes Privacy Policy

Owner: Shivam Saini

## Who we are

BadNotes is made and owned by Shivam Saini. This policy says what the app does with your data. Last updated 24 November 2026.

## Your notes

Your notes, pictures, recordings and settings are stored on this device. BadNotes has no servers of its own, does not collect them and cannot see them.

## Cloud sync (optional)

If you sign in to Google Drive or OneDrive, your notes are copied to that account so they reach your other devices. They stay in your account, under that provider's own terms and privacy policy. With Google Drive and OneDrive, BadNotes can only open the files it created itself, and reads your account's email address to show who is signed in. You can sign out at any time; files already in your cloud stay there until you delete them.

## Microphone

Used only while you record audio in a note or use voice typing. Recordings stay in your note. Voice typing uses your device's own speech service.

## Camera and files

The camera is used only when you scan a page or add a photo, and files only when you pick them to import or export. Nothing is sent anywhere by BadNotes.

## Handwriting search

Your handwriting is read on this device. The reading models are downloaded once from Google.

## Crash reports and analytics

None. BadNotes does not send crash reports, usage statistics or any other data about you or your device to anyone.

## Children

BadNotes does not knowingly collect anything from anyone, children included.

## Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version is shown here with its date. Questions: write to Shivam Saini at badnotes.co@gmail.com or see badnotes.space.
